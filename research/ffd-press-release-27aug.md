# The FFD press release of 27 August 2026 — transcribed and translated

**Read in the original on 8 September 2026.** This is the document behind the
"FFD technical report" figures that circulate in the press and in
flood-monitoring newsletters — the 6.57 m Devghat stage, the ~5,850 m³/s peak
discharge, and the "additional flood volume" that reached us as *19.96 Mm³*.
It is worth reading in full, because what it says is narrower and more careful
than what is quoted from it.

## Page 1 — the chronology

*Paragraph 1.* On the morning of 10 Bhadra 2083 (26 August 2026), after
information was received that a flood carrying high velocity, discharge and
debris had entered the Bhote Koshi river in Rasuwa district from the
Nepal–China Rasuwagadhi border, the Flood Forecasting Division has been
carrying out continuous real-time monitoring, analysis, information flow and
early-warning dissemination on the basis of available water levels, direct
information received, and the flood's travel time. From **09:15** that
morning early warning was issued to residents of the river-bank and
at-risk areas of Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading, Gorkha and Chitwan, and
**679,295 SMS** were sent through Nepal Telecom and Ncell requesting
residents of the Bhote Koshi and Trishuli river-bank areas — Timure,
Syabrubesi, Betrawati, Trishuli, Galchhi, Malekhu, Mugling, Narayanghat and
nearby settlements — to move in stages to safe locations. The flood is seen to
have passed successively down the Bhote Koshi–Trishuli, past Malekhu and
Mugling, and reached the Narayani–Devghat area.

*Paragraph 2 — the seismic record and the upper gauges.*

> खानी तथा भूगर्भ विभागबाट प्राप्त सूचना अनुसार बिहान ८:२० बजे रसुवागढी सिमा क्षेत्र
> आसपास २.६ रेक्टर स्केलको साथै बिहान ८:३७ बजे सोही स्थान आसपास ४.४ रेक्टर स्केलको
> भूकम्प मापन भएको थियो।

"According to information received from the Department of Mines and Geology,
at 08:20 in the morning near the Rasuwagadhi border area a **2.6** on the

## Page 2 — the volume, and the source

*Paragraph 1 — the sentence everything else is quoted from.*

> देवघाटमा मापन भएको बहाव विश्लेषण गर्दा १४:१० देखि १८:०० बजेसम्म आधार बहाव भन्दा
> अतिरिक्त बाढीजन्य पानीको आयतन करिब २ करोड घनमिटर रहेको प्रारम्भिक आंकलन गरिएको छ।

"On analysis of the flow measured at **Devghat**, from **14:10 to 18:00**, the
volume of flood water **additional to the base flow** is **preliminarily
estimated** at **approximately 2 crore cubic metres**."

*Paragraph 2 — the source.* Preliminary analysis of satellite imagery and data
obtained from various national and international agencies shows that a **snow
avalanche (हिमपहिरो)** occurred near the border area at
**N 28°16′50″, E 85°32′15.45″**, from which an extremely high, sudden discharge
came. The Division is at present carrying out detailed study of the glacial
lakes, glaciers and the whole snow/ice region in that area.

*Paragraph 3.* Condolences to those who died and wishes for the swift recovery
of the injured.

*Paragraph 4.* Continuous monitoring from the principal gauging stations and
assessment of the flood's effect in the lower reaches is under way; residents
of river banks, low-lying ground and potential inundation areas are asked to
stay highly alert and in safe, elevated places until further notice, to avoid
unnecessary movement near the river, and to follow the Division's information
and instructions.

## What this settles

1. **"19.96 Mm³" is not in the source.** The document says **करिब २ करोड
   घनमिटर** — *approximately* 2 crore, i.e. ~20 million, **one significant
   figure**, and expressly **प्रारम्भिक आंकलन**, a preliminary estimate. The
   two-decimal figure circulating through NESRA FloodWatch and the press is
   precision added downstream of the document.

2. **It is not a system volume balance and cannot be compared with a release
   volume.** It is the water above base flow that passed **one gauge**,
   Devghat, inside a **stated 3 h 50 min window**. It counts redistributed
   channel water that arrived inside the window, and by construction misses
   everything that arrived after 18:00. It is silent on deposition, ponding
   and unmelted ice upstream. Report §07 has had this reading since the site's
   first published version; dossier §26b records the sweep that briefly lost
   it.

3. **What it CAN constrain is the shape of the wave.** ~20 Mm³ above base over
   14:10–18:00 is a mean excess of ~1,450 m³/s; against a peak excess near
   2,900 that is a roughly triangular wave of known width. Every ensemble to
   v10b has been scored on the HEIGHT of the Devghat peak and never on its
   WIDTH. v12 reports it (`calcs/ensemble_v12.py`, integrated 333–563 min
   after 08:37, scored at one significant figure).

4. **The 08:20 M2.6 is official, not soft.** The dossier carried it as
   "[soft — Wikipedia timeline only]". It is in this release, attributed to
   the Department of Mines and Geology: a **17-minute precursor** to the 08:37
   main event, independent of the Chinese Academy of Sciences' reported
   signals at ~06:04, ~06:50 and 08:15–08:19 (§26d). Two records now support
   a staged release. Every version of our model assumes a single one.

5. **The FFD calls the source a snow avalanche**, at N 28°16′50″
   E 85°32′15.45″ — about 1.9 km east of Petley's scar coordinate, which our
   channel profile uses (`model/build_profile.py`).

6. **Our held-out arrival times come from this document.** Malekhu at 163 min
   and Kalikhola at 337 min after 08:37 are 11:20 and 14:14 — the warning and
   danger crossings above. They are held out of scoring, not independent of
   the record.
